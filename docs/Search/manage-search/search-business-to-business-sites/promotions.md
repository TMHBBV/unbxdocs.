# Overview

Query-specific merchandising rules allow you to customize promotions for specific queries or segments, offering a more targeted approach to shopper engagement.

Navigate to the Promotions section

Login to Netcore Unbxd’s self-serve console ↗

From the Site Key Picker, click the site you want to apply a merchandising strategy.

After selecting the appropriate site key, navigate to Merchandising.

Hover over Search and click Promotions.
