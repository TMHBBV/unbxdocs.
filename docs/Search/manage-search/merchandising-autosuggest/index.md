# Overview

The autosuggestion feature enhances the shopper’s search experience by offering query suggestions as they type in the search bar. It helps by

* Reducing effort and clicks to discover products.
* Correct misspelled queries and complete them automatically.
* Suggesting related products for a more comprehensive search.

Autosuggestions play a key role in guiding shoppers to relevant products on your website. Unbxd’s autosuggest feature combines autocompletion with relevancy to deliver accurate results, even correcting typos, to improve the overall shopping experience.

## Features of Autosuggest

1. **Promoted Suggestions**: Predefined query suggestions that merchants can prioritize to appear at the top of the autosuggest dropdown, driving attention to specific products or campaigns.
2. **Relevancy and Autocompletion**: Autosuggest provides relevant results while automatically correcting misspelled queries and completing search terms as users type.
3. **Blacklist Suggestions**: Merchants can exclude specific query terms from appearing in the autosuggest dropdown to avoid irrelevant or inappropriate suggestions, ensuring a refined shopping experience.
4. **Customizable Promotion and Blacklisting**: Merchants can set start and end dates for both promoted and blacklisted suggestions, giving flexibility to align with seasonal sales, promotions, or business goals.

Log in to Netcore Unbxd Search Platform. Navigate to **Merchandising** > **Autosuggest** > **Promoted Suggestions**/ **Blacklisted Suggestions**

## Blacklisted Suggestion

Blacklisted suggestions in Autosuggest are query terms that merchants choose to exclude from appearing in the Autosuggest dropdown. These terms may be removed to prevent irrelevant, outdated, or undesired suggestions from showing up, ensuring a more refined and appropriate shopping experience. Blacklisted suggestions are beneficial for:

1. Avoiding irrelevant keywords: Excluding terms that do not align with the product catalog or business goals.
2. Preventing sensitive or restricted terms: Removing words that may be inappropriate or prohibited.
3. Improving the relevance of autosuggest results: Ensuring only meaningful and shopper-relevant queries are shown.

Blacklisted suggestions will not appear in the Autosuggest dropdown if the shopper’s query includes any part of the terms you’ve blacklisted.

### Add a Blacklisted Suggestion

Navigate to the **Blacklisted Suggestions** page and click the **Add Blacklisted Suggestion** button. In the popup window, enter the terms you want to blacklist. Specify the start and end date and time for the blacklisting period (optional). Once all details are configured, click **Add** to save the blacklisted suggestion.

For Example, If you’ve blacklisted the term **Expired Deals**, it will not appear for any queries, even if the shopper types **Expired Deals** in the search bar.
