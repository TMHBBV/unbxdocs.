# Overview

The autosuggestion feature enhances the shopper’s search experience by offering query suggestions as they type in the search bar. It helps by

* Reducing effort and clicks to discover products.
* Correct misspelled queries and complete them automatically.
* Suggesting related products for a more comprehensive search.

Autosuggestions play a key role in guiding shoppers to relevant products on your website. Unbxd’s autosuggest feature combines autocompletion with relevancy to deliver accurate results, even correcting typos, to improve the overall shopping experience.

## Features of Autosuggest

1. **Promoted Suggestions**: Predefined query suggestions that merchants can prioritize to appear at the top of the autosuggest dropdown, driving attention to specific products or campaigns.
2. **Relevancy and Autocompletion**: Autosuggest provides relevant results while automatically correcting misspelled queries and completing search terms as users type.
3. **Blacklist Suggestions**: Merchants can exclude specific query terms from appearing in the autosuggest dropdown to avoid irrelevant or inappropriate suggestions, ensuring a refined shopping experience.
4. **Customizable Promotion and Blacklisting**: Merchants can set start and end dates for both promoted and blacklisted suggestions, giving flexibility to align with seasonal sales, promotions, or business goals.
