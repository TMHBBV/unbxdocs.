# Overview

Stop words are a pool of words that do not help in the ranking of results and therefore should be excluded. Ex. at, to, and, for, and so on. Prepositions and conjunctions are instances of commonly used functional words and phrases. Removing them from the search query will enhance indexing. For example: Shoes for women could simply search for ‘women shoes’ and remove the extra word ‘for’ while searching. This improves the search engine’s efficiency.

## To add stop words:

1. Navigate to Content > Stop Words.
2. Click **Add a Stopword** and add the keywords that wont add any value in searching and displaying relBulk Upload Stop Words. You can upload a list of concept terms by either browsing on your computer or by using drag and drop. The supported upload format is .csv.\
   Keep adding stop words by clicking ‘Add Stop Words’.
   Once done, save the changes.

### What should stopwords not include:

1. **Empty record**: entry where there is no value entered (whitespace)
2. **Alphanumeric**: entries with only alphanumeric values. It should have text characters.
