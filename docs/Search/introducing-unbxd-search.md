**Netcore Unbxd Search** is an AI-powered product discovery solution designed to help e-commerce platforms connect shoppers with the products they want. Curate unique experiences for every shopper and deliver highly relevant search results using natural language processing (NLP) and vector understanding.

# Key Capabilities

Make it easier for shoppers to find what they need and discover what they didn’t know they wanted.\
With Netcore Unbxd, you can deliver fast and intelligent search and browse experiences that convert casual visitors into loyal customers.

* Understand shopper intent, synonyms, misspellings, and long-tail queries with **AI-powered search**.
* Use Netcore Unbxd’s APIs, SDKs, and plug-ins to activate powerful search with minimal effort.
* Build product discovery experiences using Netcore Unbxd’s no-code console in just a few clicks.
* Customize search results, promote key products, run targeted campaigns, and manage category navigation easily.
* Control product ranking, pin or hide products, and define dynamic rules for specific campaigns.
* Access performance insights through the **conversion funnel**, **query-level data**, and **custom reports**.
* Help shoppers explore your full catalog through intelligent browse features.
* Ensure a consistent experience across devices and channels to drive loyalty and retention:

  * Location-based product search
  * Real-time inventory visibility
  * Automatically hide out-of-stock products

# Best Practices

To get the most out of Netcore Unbxd’s Search feature, follow these best practices to enhance product discovery:

* Keep product titles clear and descriptive to improve search accuracy.
* Highlight popular or seasonal items using banners or pinned results.
* Identify zero-result searches and add helpful synonyms.
* Enable auto-suggest and typo correction to simplify the search process.
* Automatically hide out-of-stock products to reduce shopper frustration.
* Group similar products under filters such as size, color, or style to improve browsing.

## Use Cases

**Use case:** How Netcore Unbxd Search improves product discovery for a bag brand

**Scenario:** Consider an e-commerce site for backpacks, totes, and travel bags.

1. A shopper searches for a **waterproof laptop bag**. Netcore Unbxd’s AI understands the intent and shows relevant products, even if the query is **laptop bag for rain**.
2. Bestsellers, new arrivals, or high-margin bags are prioritized based on the brand’s strategy.
3. The team promotes a new **Waterproof Bags Series** by pinning it to the top of search results—no developer assistance required.
4. The shopper navigates to **Travel Bags**, where filters such as size, color, and capacity automatically update to reflect real-time inventory.
5. Only in-stock products available in the shopper’s region are displayed. Out-of-stock items are automatically hidden.

**Result:** The shopper quickly finds the desired bag, and the brand delivers a seamless shopping experience across devices.
