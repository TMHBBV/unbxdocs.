We offer three methods for uploading your product catalog feed:

1. **REST APIs**: Use our custom REST APIs to upload your feed directly to our system.
2. **Secure File Transfer Protocol** (SFTP): Upload your feed via SFTP. The feed files uploaded to SFTP servers are transformed and then uploaded to Unbxd servers. SFTP supports feed files in the following formats
   * CSV
   * JSON
   * XML
3. **Platform Extensions**: Use our platform extensions for seamless feed upload. Integrate with Magento 2, Shopify, or SAP Hybris plugins to automate the catalog transfer from these platforms.
