# Search

Search lets shoppers search for products on your site quickly and easily. UNBXD makes the search intuitive. It understands typing errors, matches similar terms (like "**sneakers**" and "**running shoes**"), and shows the best results.

As the user types, the search bar gives instant suggestions. This saves time and helps users find what they’re looking for faster.

## Dynamic Ranking

Product order changes automatically based on what’s working best. For example, if a product is popular or selling fast, it moves up in the list.

## Synonym Mapping

This means connecting different words that mean the same thing. For example, “track pants” and “joggers.” It ensures both show the same products.

## Zero-Result Handling

Sometimes users search for something and find no results. UNBXD tries to fix this automatically. It shows alternatives, corrects spelling mistakes, or redirects users to other helpful pages.\
You can also review these searches and make updates to improve results.

# Recommendations

Recommendations shows product suggestions to users based on their activity. It helps increase sales by showing the right products at the right time.

Examples of recommendations:

* You may also like
* Frequently bought together
* Recommended for you
* Recently viewed

These can be shown on product pages, the cart page, home page, and more.

# Merchandising

Merchandising gives business teams more control. You can select which products to show, promote, or hide on search and listing pages.

You can:

* Pin products so they always show at the top
* Boost or lower certain brands or categories
* Set up rules for specific campaigns or seasons

# Personalization

Personalization adjusts search results and product suggestions for each user. It uses their behavior, such as what they click, view, or buy, to show more relevant products. Every shopper gets a slightly different experience based on what they are most likely to buy.

# Analytics and Reporting

UNBXD shows data on how users interact with your site. You can view:

* The search terms that convert best
* The number of users who clicked on recommendations
* Which searches gave no results
* What impact product suggestions had on sales

This helps you improve the shopping experience and increase conversions.

Key Concepts

<br />

# Product Catalog

Your product catalog is the list of all your items, including details like title, brand, price, and color. UNBXD uses this information to power search, filters, and suggestions. The cleaner your data, the better the results.

<br />

# Facets and Filters

These are the options users use to narrow down search or category results. Common filters include size, color, price range, and brand. They help users quickly find what they want.

# Boost

Boosting moves certain products higher in the list. Demoting pushes them down. This can help you promote seasonal items, new launches, or bestsellers.

# Pinned Products

Pinned products stay in a fixed position on a page. They are not affected by ranking or rules. You can use this to promote key products or top sellers.

Common Workflows\
Setting Up Search
Upload your product catalog.

Configure which product fields should be searchable (like title, brand, or category).

Set up synonyms and auto-corrections.

Enable search on your website.

Adding Product Recommendations\
Decide where you want to show recommendations like home page, product page, and so on.

Select the type of recommendation like “Frequently Bought Together”.

Enable personalization if needed.

Monitor performance and adjust as needed.

Running a Merchandising Campaign\
Define your goal like, promote new arrivals.

Set up rules to boost, pin, or hide products.

Apply the campaign to search or category pages.

Monitor results using analytics.

Adjust rules based on performance.
