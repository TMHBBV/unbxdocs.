Netcore Unbxd Recommendations is an AI-powered solution designed to deliver personalized product suggestions. By analyzing real-time shopper behavior and preferences, it provides relevant recommendations across for your, websites, mobile apps, and email campaigns.

## Key Capabilities

Enhance the shopping experience with intelligent recommendations that adapt to each shopper's journey.

* Use AI algorithms to suggest products based on individual shopper behavior and preferences.
* Deliver recommendations that align with the shopper's current context, such as browsing history or cart contents.
* Update recommendations dynamically as shopper interactions occur, ensuring relevance throughout the session.
* Maintain consistent recommendation experiences across web, mobile, and email platforms.
* Empower merchandisers with tools to customize recommendation strategies without coding.
* Access detailed reports to monitor recommendation effectiveness and optimize strategies accordingly.

## Best Practices

To maximize the impact of Netcore Unbxd Recommendations, consider the following best practices:

* Position recommendation widgets on high-traffic pages, such as homepages, product detail pages, and cart pages.
* Select appropriate recommendation algorithms such as **Bought Also Bought**,**Recently Viewed** based on the page context.
* Test different recommendation strategies to identify what works best with your audience.
* Use shopper data to give recommendations and increase engagement.
* Implement fallback options for new or anonymous users to ensure recommendations are always available

## Use Case

### Enhancing Product Discovery for an Online Apparel Store

**Scenario**: An online apparel retailer aims to increase average order value and reduce cart abandonment.

1. \*Display **Recommended for You** sections based on past browsing and purchase history.
2. Show **Customers Also Bought** and **Similar Products** to encourage user browsing on your site.
3. Introduce complementary product suggestions, such as accessories or matching items, to promote other pages
4. Send personalized emails featuring recently viewed items and product recommendations.
5. Use analytics to analyze the effectiveness of recommendation strategies and make data-driven adjustments.

**Result**: The retailer experiences increased shopper engagement, higher average order values, and a reduction in cart abandonment rates.
