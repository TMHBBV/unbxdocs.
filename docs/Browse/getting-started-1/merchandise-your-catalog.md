The Merchandising section of the console provides useful controls that will help you merchandise products in your inventory. With merchandising you can:

* Promote popular products to increase revenue ratios
* Create slots of similar categories of products to rank higher on the display page
* Configure dynamic facets
* Create specific landing-pages for festivals and occasions
* Arrange filters to narrow-down results
* With such merchandising methods, shoppers are sure to get their quick and intended products
