Netcore Unbxd Browse enhances e-commerce navigation by helping shoppers explore product catalogs easily. With smart category management and filter controls, it ensures a smooth discovery experience across devices.

# Key Capabilities

Guide your customers through your digital storefront with intelligent browsing features:

* Enable structured category navigation for easier product discovery.
* Automatically update filters like size, color, and style based on real-time inventory.
* Hide out-of-stock products to maintain a high-quality shopping experience.
* Offer consistent browsing experiences across web and mobile platforms.
* Use no-code tools to customize browse layouts and merchandising without developer help.

# Best Practices

Improve browsing efficiency and satisfaction with these best practices:

* Group similar products under filters such as size, style, colour, and so on.
* Hide unavailable products to reduce customer frustration.
* Promote seasonal collections and high-converting categories using banners or tiles.
* Ensure category pages reflect real-time inventory to prevent broken experiences.
* Maintain consistent navigation patterns across channels and devices.

# Use Case

### Enhancing Browsing for a Bag Brand

**Scenario**: A shopper navigates to the **Travel Bags** category on an e-commerce site.

1. The category page displays updated filters such as size and color based on available inventory.
2. Newly launched products are highlighted in the browse view.
3. Out-of-stock items are automatically hidden to streamline exploration.
4. Location-aware browsing ensures that only shippable products are shown for the shopper’s region.
5. The shopper browses confidently, finds a suitable bag, and completes the purchase.

**Result**: Improved product discovery through organized and dynamic navigation.
