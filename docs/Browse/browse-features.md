The Unbxd Browse allows you to personalize the browsing experience of shoppers on your site by powering pages using several signals like purchase history, product preferences, and current online behavior.

These services are easily controlled and monitored by an advanced interface called the Console. The console was built from the ground up keeping mainly merchandisers in mind and includes controls like multi-level merchandising, in-depth reporting, multi-user accessibility,etc., to help you expose your product inventory in the most optimized way.

Let’s have a look at the browse features:

# Merchandising Features

## Facets

Facets are created to help shoppers narrow down their search results. With a single query, the search results page is flooded with numerous products. But for shoppers with an intent to buy a particular product or refer to a category, facets are extremely helpful. Facets provide various categories of product attributes. Like, for a cosmetic query: brands, color, ratings are the suitable facets to let the shoppers find their product. For a dress, facets range from size, color, type, brand etc.

## Variants

Variants define various attributes of the same product. If a product type ‘dress’ is available in different colors. Then keeping the product ID same, the different colors of the product will be displayed on the search results page. So, when the shoppers hover on a product, they can see the different colors it is available in and click to add it to the cart. The variant ID of the product changes but product ID remains the same.

## Multi-Value

When the same product has different image angles, it is called having multiple values of the same product. Like if shoppers search for MACbook, they can see different images of the same product for clarity.

## Sorting

Sorting can be applied to the field name to group the products either by ascending or descending order.

## Banners / redirects

In case of an occasion, an upcoming sale, or festivity, create a relative image and display it on your site. Such eventful images draw the shoppers toward a collection of products catering to a specific category of the occasion.

## Console/Site Features

## System health / Monitoring dashboard

With our Monitoring Dashboard you can get a detailed report of the uploaded catalog and the integrated API.

## Multi language

Unbxd now provides multi-language support (French, Spanish, German, Bahasa, Italian, Swedish, Portugese, Dutch, Danish, Polish) other than just English for your eCommerce site. Now, shoppers can be browsing in French or in German but with the same Unbxd Search in the background.

# Relevancy Features

## Algorithmic ranking

Our ranking algorithms use shopper’s behaviour data on your website to optimize the ranking of the products. Our models identify the products receiving higher traction for the query and across the website and automatically rank them higher to improve conversion rate on your website. The models automatically adapt to the changing user behaviour ensuring that product ranking remains relevant at all times.

## Personalization

Showing products that cater to your shoppers’ choice is done by tracking their shopping behavior. With Unbxd AI at place, you can track your shoppers’ behavior and display personalized products to every shopper.

## SKU search

The stock keeping unit helps you identify the products in your catalog by searching for the product ID to keep a check on the inventory number.
