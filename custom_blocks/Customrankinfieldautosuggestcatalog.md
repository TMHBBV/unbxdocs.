# Q. Does Unbxd allow custom ranking of in-fields suggestions in auto-suggest?

A. The ranking of in-field suggestions is based on the analytics data received by Unbxd. Unbxd doesn’t allow the customer to define customer ranking logic for autosuggest. However, our team is working on exposing controls for the autosuggest ranking.
