---
name: FeedAPI
---
You can use our Feed APIs to upload your feed.

Three types of feed uploads we support:

1. **Full Feed Upload**: Use this method to upload your entire product catalog or to overwrite any previous copy of your feed on our servers. We recommend full feed uploads occasionally to ensure that your Unbxd catalog is in sync with the production systems.
2. **Delta Feed Upload**: Also known as Incremental Feed Upload, use this to update/add multiple records or to upload a catalog partially.
3. **Check Product Count**: Use this to verify the total number of products in your catalog before or after an upload.
